緒論：水、pH 與弱作用力

所有生化反應都發生在水裡——理解氫鍵、疏水效應、緩衝與 pKa 是踏入生物化學的第一步。

Every biochemical reaction happens in water — understanding hydrogen bonds, the hydrophobic effect, buffers and pKa is the gateway to biochemistry.

概覽

為什麼從「水」開始學生物化學？

地球上的生命建立在水的化學特性之上。水佔細胞質量的 60–70%，所有酶反應、運輸、訊息傳遞、摺疊都在水溶液中發生。水的偶極矩、氫鍵能力、高介電常數形塑了：(1) 蛋白質為什麼會摺疊；(2) 為什麼脂質會自組裝成膜；(3) 為什麼 DNA 能保持雙股螺旋；(4) 為什麼酶能催化反應。

跳過水的化學，就等於跳過了生命選擇生化反應的物理基礎。本章建立四個核心概念：氫鍵、疏水效應、緩衝、酸鹼平衡。

Life on Earth is built upon the chemistry of water. Water makes up 60–70% of cellular mass; every enzymatic reaction, transport process, signaling event, and folding pathway occurs in aqueous solution. Water's dipole moment, hydrogen-bonding capacity, and high dielectric constant shape (1) why proteins fold, (2) why lipids self-assemble into membranes, (3) why DNA maintains its double helix, and (4) why enzymes catalyze reactions.

Skip water chemistry and you skip the physical reason life chose the reactions it did. This chapter builds four cornerstone concepts: hydrogen bonds, hydrophobic effect, buffers, and acid–base equilibria.

💡
核心直覺：生命的分子幾乎全部都是「兩面派」——一邊與水交朋友（極性），一邊躲水（疏水）。「極性」與「疏水」的競爭，是摺疊、組裝、催化的能量驅力。 Core intuition: Almost every biological molecule is a "two-faced" entity — part friendly to water (polar), part hiding from it (hydrophobic). The competition between polarity and hydrophobicity is the energetic driving force behind folding, assembly, and catalysis.
水的化學

一、水分子的四個關鍵性質

💧

極性與偶極矩

O 的電負度 (3.44) 遠高於 H (2.20)，O–H 鍵帶有部分電荷：O δ⁻、H δ⁺。淨偶極矩 1.85 D，使水分子間能形成強氫鍵。

O (electronegativity 3.44) is much more electronegative than H (2.20). O–H bonds carry partial charges: O δ⁻, H δ⁺. Net dipole moment 1.85 D allows water to form strong hydrogen bonds.

🔗

氫鍵網絡

每個水分子平均同時形成 ~3.4 個氫鍵（液態 25°C）。鍵能 ~20 kJ/mol，遠弱於共價鍵 (~400) 但遠強於 van der Waals (~1)。生命剛好用對了這種「中等強度」的力。

Each water molecule forms ~3.4 hydrogen bonds on average (liquid, 25°C). Bond energy ~20 kJ/mol — far weaker than covalent (~400) but far stronger than van der Waals (~1). Life works at exactly this "Goldilocks" energy.

⚡

高介電常數

水能屏蔽帶電基團的庫倫力 78 倍——這就是為什麼 NaCl 在水裡溶解，但鹽橋（蛋白內離子鍵）在蛋白質疏水內部（ε ≈ 4–10）卻變得很強。

Water shields charge–charge Coulomb forces by ~78× — that's why NaCl dissolves in water, but salt bridges become much stronger in protein hydrophobic interiors (ε ≈ 4–10).

🌀

自解離

2 H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + OH⁻，Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ (25°C)。中性水 pH = 7 並非「沒有質子」，而是 [H⁺] = 10⁻⁷ M。生理 pH 7.4 略微鹼性。

2 H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + OH⁻, with Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ at 25°C. "Neutral" pH 7 isn't proton-free — it just means [H⁺] = 10⁻⁷ M. Physiological pH 7.4 is slightly basic.

弱作用力

二、四種「弱」非共價作用力

蛋白質摺疊、DNA 雙螺旋、藥物-靶點結合，都靠這四種作用力的加和——個別很弱（1–20 kJ/mol），但成百上千個累加起來就足以維持結構。

Protein folding, DNA double helix, drug–target binding all rely on the summation of these four forces — individually weak (1–20 kJ/mol), but hundreds or thousands of them are enough to hold structure.

⚠️
「疏水效應」不是作用力——是熵效應。非極性分子聚在一起，並非彼此吸引，而是讓水分子重新獲得熵（不再需要排列成籠狀包覆它們）。這是熱力學最深的反直覺之一。 The "hydrophobic effect" is not a force — it's an entropy effect. Nonpolar molecules cluster together not because they attract each other, but because doing so releases water molecules from forming cage-like clathrates around them, raising the system's entropy. One of thermodynamics's deepest counter-intuitions.

決策引導

三、緩衝液選擇決策

🌳 緩衝液選擇決策流程

Q1:
目標 pH 是多少？→ 選擇 pKa 在目標 pH ± 1 內的緩衝對。
Q2:
需要做酶反應嗎？→ 避開會螯合金屬離子或干擾酶的緩衝（如 Tris 不適合金屬酶）。改用 HEPES、MOPS、PIPES（Good's buffers）。
Q3:
溫度變化大嗎？→ Tris 的 pKa 對溫度極敏感 (ΔpKa/ΔT ≈ −0.028)，4°C 與 37°C 可差 0.9 pH 單位！改用 phosphate 或 HEPES。
Q4:
分光光度分析？→ 確認緩衝在你的波長下不吸光（HEPES 在 230 nm 以下吸光，蛋白 UV 量測會受干擾）。
Q1:
What is your target pH? → Pick a buffer whose pKa lies within target pH ± 1.
Q2:
Running enzymatic reactions? → Avoid buffers that chelate metals or interfere with the enzyme (Tris is bad for metalloenzymes). Use HEPES, MOPS, PIPES (Good's buffers).
Q3:
Wide temperature range? → Tris pKa is extremely temperature-sensitive (ΔpKa/ΔT ≈ −0.028); can shift 0.9 pH units between 4°C and 37°C! Use phosphate or HEPES.
Q4:
Spectrophotometric assay? → Confirm the buffer is transparent at your wavelength (HEPES absorbs below 230 nm — interferes with protein UV measurements).
生理應用

四、血液 pH 的三層防禦

碳酸-碳酸氫鹽緩衝（毫秒）

CO₂ + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻。pKa = 6.1，但因為 [HCO₃⁻]/[CO₂] ≈ 20，仍能在 pH 7.4 提供緩衝。關鍵：這個系統是「開放」的——肺可調節 CO₂ 排出量、腎可調節 HCO₃⁻ 重吸收。

CO₂ + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻. pKa = 6.1, but because [HCO₃⁻]/[CO₂] ≈ 20, it buffers effectively at pH 7.4. Key: this is an "open" system — lungs adjust CO₂ exhalation, kidneys adjust HCO₃⁻ reabsorption.

呼吸代償（分鐘）

血液 pH 下降 → 化學感受器刺激呼吸 → 過度通氣 → CO₂ 排出 → pH 上升。臨床上常見於糖尿病酮酸中毒患者的「Kussmaul 呼吸」。

Falling pH → chemoreceptors trigger respiration → hyperventilation → CO₂ exhaled → pH rises. Clinically, this is "Kussmaul breathing" seen in diabetic ketoacidosis.

腎臟代償（小時–天）

腎小管調節 H⁺ 排泄與 HCO₃⁻ 重吸收。慢但最終決定酸鹼平衡。腎衰竭患者代謝性酸中毒，正是這層防線失效。

Renal tubules regulate H⁺ secretion and HCO₃⁻ reabsorption. Slow but ultimately determines acid–base balance. Metabolic acidosis in renal failure reflects loss of this defense.

🩸
臨床警示：血液 pH 偏離 7.35–7.45 區間超過 0.05 即為臨床急症。pH < 7.0 或 > 7.7 通常致命——蛋白質會在這個範圍外大規模變性、酶活喪失。 Clinical alert: Blood pH deviating >0.05 from the 7.35–7.45 range is a medical emergency. pH < 7.0 or > 7.7 is usually fatal — proteins denature en masse and enzymes lose activity outside this window.
程式碼

Henderson–Hasselbalch 計算

# pH = pKa + log10([A-]/[HA])
# 已知 pKa 與目標 pH，計算 A-/HA 比例
hh_ratio <- function(pH, pKa) 10^(pH - pKa)

# 範例：磷酸緩衝 pKa = 7.2，目標 pH = 7.4
hh_ratio(7.4, 7.2)  # 1.585 → 配 HPO4²⁻ : H2PO4⁻ ≈ 1.59 : 1

# 100 mM 總緩衝濃度的兩元素
total <- 100
ratio <- hh_ratio(7.4, 7.2)
HA <- total / (1 + ratio); A <- total - HA
cat("H2PO4-:", HA, "mM, HPO4(2-):", A, "mM\n")

# 緩衝容量 β (Van Slyke)
beta <- function(C, pH, pKa) {
  Ka <- 10^(-pKa); H <- 10^(-pH)
  2.303 * C * Ka * H / (Ka + H)^2
}
beta(0.1, 7.4, 7.2)  # 最大 β 在 pH = pKa
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

def hh_ratio(pH, pKa):
    return 10**(pH - pKa)

# 磷酸緩衝設計
total = 100  # mM
pH, pKa = 7.4, 7.2
ratio = hh_ratio(pH, pKa)
HA = total / (1 + ratio)
A  = total - HA
print(f"H2PO4-: {HA:.1f} mM, HPO4(2-): {A:.1f} mM")

# 緩衝容量曲線
pH_arr = np.linspace(3, 11, 200)
def beta(C, pH, pKa):
    Ka = 10**(-pKa); H = 10**(-pH)
    return 2.303 * C * Ka * H / (Ka + H)**2

plt.plot(pH_arr, beta(0.1, pH_arr, 7.2))
plt.xlabel("pH"); plt.ylabel("β (M/pH)")
plt.axvline(7.2, ls="--", color="crimson", label="pKa")
plt.legend(); plt.show()
