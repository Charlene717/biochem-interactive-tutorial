CHAPTER 15 / 18 · BLOCK D

中心法則的化學細節

DNA 複製、轉錄、轉譯、PTM 全部都是化學反應——這章把 Block A-D 串起來。

DNA replication, transcription, translation, PTMs are all chemistry — this chapter weaves Block A-D together.

複製

一、DNA 複製化學

DNA pol III (E. coli) 或 pol α/δ/ε (eukaryote) 催化核苷酸聚合反應：3'-OH 攻擊新核苷酸的 α-磷酸，焦磷酸 (PPi) 為離去基團。每加一個核苷酸消耗約 6 ATP eq。

三層保真機制：

  • 基質選擇性：聚合酶口袋形狀讓正確 dNTP 結合最緊（錯誤率 ~10⁻⁴）
  • 3'→5' 校對 exonuclease：剛加入錯誤核苷酸時切除（降至 10⁻⁶）
  • 錯配修復 (MMR)：複製後識別並修正（降至 10⁻⁹）

端粒問題：線性染色體末端因為「最後一個 RNA 引子無法移除」會逐漸縮短。端粒酶 (telomerase) 是 RNA 模板的反轉錄酶，可加長端粒（生殖細胞、幹細胞、85% 癌細胞中活化）——細胞老化與癌的化學基礎。

DNA pol III (E. coli) or pol α/δ/ε (eukaryote) catalyze nucleotide polymerization: 3'-OH attacks the α-phosphate of the incoming nucleotide; pyrophosphate (PPi) leaves. Each addition costs ~6 ATP equivalents.

Three-layer fidelity:

  • Substrate selectivity: polymerase pocket binds correct dNTP tightest (~10⁻⁴ error rate)
  • 3'→5' proofreading exonuclease: removes mismatches just incorporated (→ 10⁻⁶)
  • Mismatch repair (MMR): post-replicative correction (→ 10⁻⁹)

The telomere problem: linear chromosomes shorten because the final RNA primer can't be removed. Telomerase is an RNA-templated reverse transcriptase that extends telomeres (active in germline, stem cells, and 85% of cancers) — the chemistry behind aging and oncogenesis.

轉錄

二、轉錄化學

真核細胞有三個 RNA polymerase：I (rRNA), II (mRNA + 多數 ncRNA), III (tRNA + 5S rRNA)。Pol II 的活位點與 DNA pol 結構類似，催化機制也類似——但沒有校對，所以 RNA 錯誤率 ~10⁻⁵（比 DNA 高 10000 倍，但 RNA 不需傳代）。

真核 mRNA 加工三大事件：

  • 5' cap：m7G 帽，由 3 個酶串聯加上。保護 mRNA、招募核糖體
  • 剪接 (splicing)：剪接體（U1-U6 snRNAs + ~150 蛋白）切除 intron。這個反應的催化中心是 RNA（U2/U6 snRNA），是 ribozyme
  • 3' polyA tail：~200 個 A，保護降解、出核、翻譯效率

替代剪接讓人類 ~95% 基因產生多種異構物——一個基因可表達數十種蛋白。

Eukaryotes have three RNA polymerases: I (rRNA), II (mRNA + most ncRNA), III (tRNA + 5S rRNA). Pol II's active site resembles DNA pol's, with similar two-metal catalysis — but no proofreading, so RNA error rate ~10⁻⁵ (10000× higher than DNA, but RNA isn't inherited).

Three major mRNA processing events in eukaryotes:

  • 5' cap: m7G cap added by three enzymes in series. Protects mRNA, recruits ribosomes
  • Splicing: spliceosome (U1-U6 snRNAs + ~150 proteins) removes introns. The catalytic center is RNA (U2/U6 snRNA) — a ribozyme
  • 3' polyA tail: ~200 A's, protects from degradation, aids export, boosts translation

Alternative splicing lets ~95% of human genes produce multiple isoforms — one gene can encode dozens of proteins.

轉譯

三、轉譯化學

核糖體 = 大小兩個次單元（細菌 50S/30S, 真核 60S/40S）。其中肽基轉移酶活性 (peptidyl transferase center, PTC) 完全由 23S/28S rRNA 催化——核糖體是ribozyme（Cech, Steitz, Yonath 等獲 2009 Nobel）。

三個位點 A, P, E：(1) tRNA-AA 進 A 位點；(2) P 位點上的肽鏈攻擊新 AA 的氨基；(3) 肽鏈轉到 A 位點 tRNA；(4) 核糖體前移，A→P、P→E、E→空。

tRNA 載胺基酸：由 20 個 aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase 完成（每個 AA 一種酶）。這是遺傳密碼真正落實的化學步驟——錯誤率必須 <10⁻⁴，否則蛋白質序列就亂了。許多 synthetase 帶有「校對結構域」剔除錯誤載入的 AA。

抗生素標靶：許多抗生素針對細菌核糖體（人類粒線體核糖體較像細菌，有副作用風險）。四環素阻 A 位點；氯黴素抑 PTC；大環內酯 (erythromycin) 卡住出口隧道。

The ribosome = two subunits (bacterial 50S/30S, eukaryotic 60S/40S). Peptidyl transferase activity (PTC) is fully catalyzed by 23S/28S rRNA — the ribosome is a ribozyme (Cech, Steitz, Yonath, 2009 Nobel).

Three sites A, P, E: (1) aminoacyl-tRNA enters A site; (2) growing peptide on P-site attacks the incoming AA's amino group; (3) chain transfers to A-site tRNA; (4) ribosome translocates: A→P, P→E, E→empty.

tRNA charging: done by 20 aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases (one per AA). This is the chemical step where the genetic code is actually enforced — error rate must be <10⁻⁴ or proteins are garbled. Many synthetases have proofreading domains to reject mischarged AAs.

Antibiotic targets: many antibiotics target bacterial ribosomes (human mitochondrial ribosomes resemble bacterial, hence side-effect risk). Tetracyclines block A site; chloramphenicol inhibits PTC; macrolides (erythromycin) jam the exit tunnel.

⚠️
PTM 大爆炸：單一蛋白可同時帶數十種 PTM，組合稱「PTM 程式碼 (PTM code)」。組蛋白尾部的 PTM 組合構成「histone code」直接影響染色質狀態與基因表達——表觀遺傳學的化學基礎。 PTM combinatorial explosion: a single protein can carry dozens of PTMs; the combination is called the "PTM code." Histone-tail PTM combinations form the histone code, directly controlling chromatin state and gene expression — the chemical foundation of epigenetics.

# mRNA 疫苗 codon 優化思路
# 1) 提高 GC% → mRNA 穩定性 + 翻譯效率
# 2) 移除 ARE (AU-rich element)、minor codons
# 3) 替換 U → m1Ψ (Karikó & Weissman 2023 Nobel)

def codon_optimize(protein_seq, preferred_codons):
    return "".join([preferred_codons[aa] for aa in protein_seq])

# 預測核糖體暫停（從 Ribo-seq 數據）
# Pause sites: 罕見密碼子叢集、prolinerich
def find_pause_sites(cds, rare_codon_freq):
    pauses = []
    for i in range(0, len(cds)-14, 3):
        window = [cds[i+j:i+j+3] for j in range(0, 15, 3)]
        rare_count = sum(1 for c in window if rare_codon_freq.get(c, 1) < 0.3)
        if rare_count >= 3:
            pauses.append(i)
    return pauses
