Ch.1-2 · Lehninger 8e (2021) 與 Berg 10e (2023) 術語差異

原教學在水、pH、胺基酸等基礎章節混用 Lehninger 與 Berg 兩大教科書的術語。實務上兩者並非完全等價：Lehninger 8e（Nelson & Cox 2021）使用 “weak interactions” 統稱氫鍵、離子作用、van der Waals、疏水效應；Berg 10e（Berg, Tymoczko, Gatto, Stryer 2023）則習慣分述為 “noncovalent bonds / forces”。同樣地，Lehninger 將疏水效應描述為 entropy-driven，Berg 在第 1 章強調 水分子排序損失。讀者比對兩書時應留意章節編號（Lehninger Ch.2 vs Berg Ch.2）與圖示方向（Lehninger 由結構→功能；Berg 由作用力→結構）。

Ch.7 · K_M / V_max 與 k_cat / K_M 的解讀差異

原教學在動力學章節並列 K_M、V_max 與 k_cat / K_M 三個量，但學生最常見的誤解是把 K_M 等同於受質親和力（K_d）。嚴格而言，K_M = (k_-1 + k_cat) / k_1，只有在 k_cat ≪ k_-1（快速平衡近似）時 K_M ≈ K_d；當 k_cat 接近或大於 k_-1（如 carbonic anhydrase、超氧化物歧化酶等近擴散極限酶）時，K_M 顯著大於 K_d。比較不同酶或不同受質的「催化效率」時，必須用 k_cat / K_M（specificity constant），而不是單看 V_max — 因為 V_max 同時依賴於 [E]_total 與 k_cat，無法在不同酶之間直接比較；k_cat / K_M 的上限即擴散極限 ~10^8–10^9 M⁻¹ s⁻¹（Fersht Structure and Mechanism in Protein Science, 1999；Bar-Even et al., Biochemistry 50:4402, 2011, DOI）。

The kinetics chapter introduces K_M, V_max, and k_cat / K_M side by side, but the most common student misconception is to equate K_M with substrate affinity (K_d). Strictly, K_M = (k_-1 + k_cat) / k_1, and K_M ≈ K_d only under the rapid-equilibrium approximation when k_cat ≪ k_-1. For near-diffusion-limited enzymes (carbonic anhydrase, superoxide dismutase) k_cat ≳ k_-1, so K_M substantially exceeds K_d. To compare catalytic efficiency across enzymes or substrates, use the specificity constant k_cat / K_M, not V_max — V_max depends on [E]_total and is not enzyme-agnostic; the diffusion limit caps k_cat / K_M near 10^8–10^9 M⁻¹ s⁻¹ (Fersht, Structure and Mechanism in Protein Science, 1999; Bar-Even et al., Biochemistry 50:4402, 2011, DOI).

Ch.8 · Hill 係數 ≠ 異位調控的證據（MWC 1965 vs KNF 1966）

原教學以血紅蛋白 S-shaped 曲線示範 Hill 係數 n_H ≈ 2.8，並將其等同於「具有 allosteric 調控」。這是常見但錯誤的捷徑。Hill 係數只是 log[Y/(1−Y)] vs log[S] 斜率的經驗值，不等於協同性的機制，更不等於亞基數目。理論上：(1) n_H = 1 不代表沒有 allostery（許多 V 型 allosteric 酶在飽和受質下 n_H ≈ 1）；(2) n_H 上限是亞基數，但實測值幾乎總是低於該上限（Hb 四聚體理論上限 4，實測 ~2.8）；(3) MWC（Monod, Wyman, Changeux 1965, J. Mol. Biol. 12:88，DOI）假設所有亞基同步在 T↔R 之間切換；KNF（Koshland, Némethy, Filmer 1966, Biochemistry 5:365，DOI）允許序列性誘導契合，兩模型給出相似 S-曲線卻機制完全不同。實務上應同時報告 n_H、K_0.5（不是 K_M！）與配體結合化學計量。

The chapter uses the hemoglobin S-shaped curve to show n_H ≈ 2.8 and equates this with "allosteric regulation." This shortcut is common but wrong. The Hill coefficient is just an empirical slope of log[Y/(1−Y)] vs log[S]; it is neither a mechanism for cooperativity nor the number of subunits. (1) n_H = 1 does not mean "no allostery" — many V-type allosteric enzymes show n_H ≈ 1 under saturating substrate; (2) the theoretical ceiling of n_H is the subunit count but observed values almost always fall below it (Hb tetramer ceiling 4, observed ~2.8); (3) MWC (Monod, Wyman, Changeux 1965, J. Mol. Biol. 12:88, DOI) assumes all subunits switch concertedly between T↔R, while KNF (Koshland, Némethy, Filmer 1966, Biochemistry 5:365, DOI) allows sequential induced fit — both fit similar sigmoidal curves with completely different mechanisms. Best practice: report n_H, K_0.5 (not K_M!), and ligand stoichiometry together.

Ch.9, 15 · ATP / GTP / NADPH 的分工 — 別把所有「高能磷酸鍵」混為一談

原教學偶爾將 ATP / GTP / NADPH 一起稱作「細胞能量貨幣」。實際上三者各有專責，不可隨意互換：(1) ATP 是通用磷酸基團轉移者（ΔG°' ≈ −30.5 kJ/mol，hydrolysis），驅動絕大多數激酶反應與肌肉收縮；(2) GTP 主要用於蛋白合成（translation EF-Tu/EF-G、釋放因子）、訊息傳導（G proteins、Ras GTPase 計時器）與微管動力學 — 這些反應對 GTP 有結構特異性，無法以 ATP 替代；(3) NADPH 不是磷酸基團供體而是還原力貨幣，主要用於脂肪酸 / 膽固醇生合成、解毒（GSH 再生）與抗氧化系統，胞內 [NADPH]/[NADP⁺] 比值遠高於 [NADH]/[NAD⁺]（典型 ~100 vs ~0.01；Veech et al., Biochem J 1969；Hosios & Vander Heiden, Mol Cell 2018, DOI）。教學中應強調「能量」（磷酸基團 vs 還原當量）與「特異性」（哪個酶用哪個核苷酸）兩個軸。

The tutorial sometimes lumps ATP / GTP / NADPH together as "the cell's energy currency." In fact each has a specialised role and they are not interchangeable: (1) ATP is the universal phosphoryl-transfer donor (ΔG°' ≈ −30.5 kJ/mol of hydrolysis), powering most kinase reactions and muscle contraction; (2) GTP drives protein synthesis (translation EF-Tu/EF-G, release factors), signal transduction (G proteins, Ras GTPase timers) and microtubule dynamics — these reactions show structural specificity for GTP and cannot use ATP; (3) NADPH is not a phosphoryl donor but a reducing-power currency, supplying lipid/cholesterol biosynthesis, detoxification (GSH regeneration) and antioxidant defence; the cellular [NADPH]/[NADP⁺] ratio is far higher than [NADH]/[NAD⁺] (≈ 100 vs ≈ 0.01; Veech et al., Biochem J 1969; Hosios & Vander Heiden, Mol Cell 2018, DOI). Teaching should separate two axes: "energy form" (phosphoryl vs reducing equivalents) and "specificity" (which enzyme uses which nucleotide).

Ch.3, 18 · AlphaFold 3 的非商用授權限制

原教學在介紹 AlphaFold 3（Abramson et al. 2024, Nature 630:493, DOI）時著重在準確度與配體 / DNA / RNA 預測能力，但並未提醒讀者授權條款。AF3 與 AF2 截然不同：(1) AF2（2021）以 Apache 2.0 開源，模型權重可下載並商用；(2) AF3（2024）初始僅以 AlphaFold Server（alphafoldserver.com）線上提供，禁止商用、禁止訓練衍生模型、每日有 query 上限；(3) 2024 年 11 月 DeepMind 在 GitHub 公開推理 code 但權重仍須申請、且僅限學術非營利使用；(4) 對藥廠 / 商業 CRO 而言，這意味著結構若用於專利、藥物開發或商業 services 都不能直接使用 AF3 輸出；社群已轉向 Boltz-1（MIT lab, 2024, bioRxiv）與 Chai-1 等開源替代。引用 AF3 結構時應註明來源與授權範圍。

When introducing AlphaFold 3 (Abramson et al. 2024, Nature 630:493, DOI) the tutorial emphasises accuracy and ligand/DNA/RNA prediction but does not flag the licence. AF3 differs sharply from AF2: (1) AF2 (2021) is Apache-2.0 open source with downloadable weights usable commercially; (2) AF3 (2024) was initially available only through AlphaFold Server (alphafoldserver.com) with non-commercial terms, no derivative training, and daily query caps; (3) in November 2024 DeepMind released inference code on GitHub, but weights still require an application and are restricted to academic non-profit use; (4) for pharma / commercial CROs this means AF3 outputs cannot be used directly for patents, drug development, or paid services — the community has moved to open alternatives such as Boltz-1 (MIT lab, 2024, bioRxiv) and Chai-1. Always cite AF3 structures with their licence scope.

Ch.16 · Casgevy（2023 FDA 核准）的真實機制 — BCL11A enhancer，不是直接編輯 HBB

原教學在臨床章節介紹 Casgevy（exa-cel）為「全球首個 CRISPR-Cas9 核准療法」，但學生常誤以為它「修復 HBB 鐮刀型突變」。實際機制完全不同：Casgevy 不修改鐮刀型 β-globin (HBB E6V) 突變，而是用 Cas9 切割紅血球祖細胞 BCL11A 基因的紅系特異性 enhancer（+58 區），破壞 BCL11A 在紅血球譜系的表達。BCL11A 是胎兒血紅蛋白 (HbF, γ-globin) 的關鍵抑制者；其敲低使成年患者重新表達 HbF，HbF 不含突變的 β-globin、可正常攜氧並抑制 HbS 聚合（Bauer et al., Science 342:253, 2013, DOI；Frangoul et al., NEJM 384:252, 2021, DOI）。因此 Casgevy 同時治療鐮刀型貧血與 β-thalassemia — 兩者都受益於 HbF reactivation。這也說明「editing the disease gene」與「editing a regulator of the disease gene」是兩種完全不同的策略。

The clinical chapter calls Casgevy (exa-cel) "the world's first CRISPR-Cas9–approved therapy" but students often assume it "repairs the HBB sickle mutation." It does not. Casgevy does not edit the sickle β-globin (HBB E6V) mutation; instead Cas9 cuts the erythroid-specific enhancer of BCL11A (the +58 region) in haematopoietic progenitors, disrupting BCL11A expression in the red-cell lineage. BCL11A is the master repressor of fetal haemoglobin (HbF, γ-globin); its knockdown reactivates HbF in adult patients, and HbF lacks the mutant β-globin, carries oxygen normally, and blocks HbS polymerisation (Bauer et al., Science 342:253, 2013, DOI; Frangoul et al., NEJM 384:252, 2021, DOI). The same mechanism therefore treats both sickle-cell disease and β-thalassemia, both of which benefit from HbF reactivation. Conceptually: "editing the disease gene" and "editing a regulator of the disease gene" are two distinct therapeutic strategies.

Ch.4, 17 · Ro5 vs bRo5 — PROTAC 與大環化合物已突破傳統 drug-likeness

原教學引用 Lipinski's Rule of 5 (Ro5)（Lipinski et al. 2001, Adv Drug Deliv Rev 46:3，DOI）做為小分子藥物設計的指導原則：MW < 500, logP < 5, HBD ≤ 5, HBA ≤ 10。但讀者應知道 Ro5 在 PROTAC、molecular glue 與某些大環化合物上完全不適用。Doak et al.（2014, Chem Biol 21:1115，DOI）提出 "beyond Rule of 5" (bRo5) 空間（MW 500–3000, logP 高達 10, HBD/HBA 更寬鬆），涵蓋環孢素 A、口服雷帕霉素類似物與多數 PROTAC（MW ~800–1200）。臨床上已有口服 PROTAC（ARV-471/vepdegestrant、ARV-110/bavdegalutamide）進入後期試驗，證明 bRo5 化合物可獲得有用的口服生物利用度（Mullard, Nat Rev Drug Discov 20:247, 2021，DOI；DeGoey et al., J Med Chem 61:2636, 2018，DOI）。教學應同時呈現 Ro5（小分子起點）與 bRo5（PROTAC / 大環）兩個座標系。

The tutorial cites Lipinski's Rule of 5 (Ro5) (Lipinski et al. 2001, Adv Drug Deliv Rev 46:3, DOI): MW < 500, logP < 5, HBD ≤ 5, HBA ≤ 10 — but readers should know Ro5 does not apply to PROTACs, molecular glues, or many macrocycles. Doak et al. (2014, Chem Biol 21:1115, DOI) defined the "beyond Rule of 5" (bRo5) space (MW 500–3000, logP up to 10, looser HBD/HBA limits) covering cyclosporin A, oral rapamycin analogues, and most PROTACs (MW ~800–1200). Oral PROTACs (ARV-471/vepdegestrant, ARV-110/bavdegalutamide) are now in late-stage trials, proving bRo5 molecules can deliver useful oral bioavailability (Mullard, Nat Rev Drug Discov 20:247, 2021, DOI; DeGoey et al., J Med Chem 61:2636, 2018, DOI). Teach Ro5 (small-molecule starting point) and bRo5 (PROTAC / macrocycle) as two coordinate systems, not one universal rule.

Ch.10, 16 · Warburg 效應的現代修正（Vander Heiden 2017）

原教學在醣解 / 癌症代謝章節引用 Warburg (1956, Science 123:309) 的「癌細胞偏好有氧醣解 (aerobic glycolysis)」觀察，但若僅停留在「線粒體缺陷」的舊解釋將造成嚴重誤導。現代共識（Vander Heiden, Cantley & Thompson 2009, Science 324:1029，DOI；Liberti & Locasale 2016, Trends Biochem Sci 41:211，DOI；DeBerardinis & Chandel 2020, Sci Adv 6:eaaz0463，DOI）已將 Warburg 效應重新詮釋為：(1) 多數癌細胞的線粒體功能正常，TCA 循環仍主動，氧化磷酸化也持續運作 — Warburg 的「mitochondrial damage causes cancer」假設大部分錯誤；(2) 偏好醣解的真正原因是滿足快速增殖所需的合成代謝（biosynthetic）需求：醣解中間產物分流進入 PPP（核苷酸合成）、絲胺酸 / 甘胺酸途徑（一碳代謝）、甘油 / 脂質骨架；(3) 此外，乳酸鹽本身是訊息分子與燃料（Faubert et al., Cell 171:358, 2017，DOI），可被其他癌細胞 / 基質細胞輸入回 TCA。教學中宜以「增殖代謝重編程」取代過時的「線粒體缺陷」敘述。

The glycolysis / cancer-metabolism chapter cites Warburg's (1956, Science 123:309) observation that tumours prefer aerobic glycolysis, but stopping at the old "broken mitochondria" interpretation is seriously misleading. The modern consensus (Vander Heiden, Cantley & Thompson 2009, Science 324:1029, DOI; Liberti & Locasale 2016, Trends Biochem Sci 41:211, DOI; DeBerardinis & Chandel 2020, Sci Adv 6:eaaz0463, DOI) reinterprets the Warburg effect: (1) most tumour cells have functional mitochondria, with active TCA flux and ongoing oxidative phosphorylation — Warburg's "mitochondrial damage causes cancer" hypothesis is largely wrong; (2) the real driver of glycolytic preference is biosynthetic demand for rapid proliferation: glycolytic intermediates branch into PPP (nucleotides), serine/glycine (one-carbon metabolism), and glycerol/lipid backbones; (3) lactate itself is a signalling molecule and a fuel (Faubert et al., Cell 171:358, 2017, DOI) — neighbouring tumour or stromal cells can import lactate back into the TCA cycle. Replace the obsolete "mitochondrial defect" framing with "proliferative metabolic reprogramming."
