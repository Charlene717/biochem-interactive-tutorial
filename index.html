生物化學互動式教程

化學如何成為生命的語言——18 章雙語教程，從水、胺基酸與酶動力學，到代謝整合、訊息傳遞、AlphaFold 3 結構與 2025 藥物設計，串起經典生化、臨床應用與計算生物學三條主線。

How chemistry becomes the language of life — 18 bilingual chapters from water, amino acids and enzyme kinetics through metabolic integration, signaling, AlphaFold 3 structural biology and 2025 drug-design advances. Three threads woven together: classical biochemistry, clinical medicine, and computational biology.
