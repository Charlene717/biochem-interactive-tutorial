SBDD

二、SBDD

SBDD 流程：(1) 取得目標蛋白結構（X-ray, cryo-EM, NMR, 或 AlphaFold 3 預測）；(2) 找出結合口袋；(3) 虛擬篩選或片段篩選找 hit；(4) 結構-活性關係 (SAR) 優化；(5) 改善 ADMET（藥動學）；(6) 進臨床測試。

2024 AlphaFold 3 的影響：AF3 對蛋白-小分子複合物預測比傳統 docking 提升 ~50%（PoseBusters benchmark）。但並非取代實驗結構——預測幾何不等於可信結合自由能，仍需實驗驗證。AF3 加速了「初步篩選 + 假設生成」，而非取代藥物開發後段。

分子對接 (docking)：將小分子放入口袋，評估配合度（AutoDock Vina, Glide, GOLD）。標準流程用 ROC AUC, RMSD < 2Å 評估。

Fragment-Based Drug Discovery (FBDD)：從小片段（MW < 250）開始篩選——比 HTS 的命中率高 100×。透過 X-ray 或 NMR (STD, WaterLOGSY) 偵測弱結合 (mM-μM Kd)，再串接/生長。已誕生多個臨床藥物（venetoclax, vemurafenib）。

SBDD pipeline: (1) obtain target structure (X-ray, cryo-EM, NMR, or AlphaFold 3); (2) identify binding pocket; (3) virtual screen or fragment screen for hits; (4) SAR optimization; (5) improve ADMET; (6) clinical trials.

2024 AF3 impact: AF3 protein-ligand prediction is ~50% better than traditional docking (PoseBusters benchmark). But it does not replace experimental structures — predicted geometry doesn't equal reliable binding ΔG; experimental validation still needed. AF3 accelerates "initial screen + hypothesis generation," not late-stage drug discovery.

Docking: place a small molecule in a pocket and score the fit (AutoDock Vina, Glide, GOLD). Standard QC uses ROC AUC and RMSD < 2 Å.

Fragment-Based Drug Discovery (FBDD): start from tiny fragments (MW < 250); hit rates are 100× higher than HTS. Detect weak binding (mM-μM Kd) via X-ray or NMR (STD, WaterLOGSY), then link/grow. Multiple clinical drugs from FBDD (venetoclax, vemurafenib).

共價

三、共價抑制劑

過去因擔心毒性（off-target 共價反應）共價藥物被冷落，但 2010 後熱潮復興——關鍵是精準選擇 warhead 化學 + 靶向 Cys（cysteine 比 Lys 親核性強，且基因組可定位）。

標誌性 2021 突破：sotorasib (AMG-510, Lumakras)，第一個 KRAS-G12C 共價抑制劑。KRAS 過去被認為「不可成藥」（口袋淺、平滑），sotorasib 利用 G12C 突變產生的獨特 Cys 殘基形成共價鍵。一年後 adagrasib 跟進。這扇門打開後，多個「不可成藥」靶點開始被攻克。

2024-2025 趨勢：(1) 共價片段篩選；(2) 反向化學基因組學 (chemogenomics) 找 cysteinome 可成藥靶點；(3) photoswitchable 共價 warhead；(4) 共價結合到 Lys、Tyr、Ser（非僅 Cys）。

Covalent drugs were long avoided due to off-target toxicity, but post-2010 a renaissance has emerged — driven by carefully chosen warhead chemistry + targeting Cys (more nucleophilic than Lys, and genomically mappable).

Landmark 2021 breakthrough: sotorasib (AMG-510, Lumakras), the first KRAS-G12C covalent inhibitor. KRAS was considered "undruggable" (shallow, smooth pocket); sotorasib exploits the unique Cys created by the G12C mutation to form a covalent bond. Adagrasib followed a year later. With this door open, many "undruggable" targets are being attacked.

2024-2025 trends: (1) covalent fragment screening; (2) chemogenomics to map druggable cysteinome targets; (3) photoswitchable covalent warheads; (4) targeting Lys, Tyr, Ser (not just Cys).

PROTAC

四、PROTAC

傳統抑制劑透過佔據（occupancy）阻斷功能；TPD 則透過事件驅動（event-driven）讓細胞自己降解標靶。

PROTAC（Proteolysis-Targeting Chimera）：雙頭分子——一端結合標靶蛋白、另一端結合 E3 連接酶（cereblon、VHL）→ 拉近距離 → 標靶被泛素化 → 蛋白酶體降解。一個 PROTAC 分子可催化多次降解（催化型）。

2025 最新發展：

  • Trivalent PROTACs：三點結合，三元複合物更穩定，降解效率更高
  • 新 E3 連接酶：超越 cereblon / VHL，拓展到 RNF114、KEAP1、FEM1B 等
  • Photoswitchable PROTACs (PHOTAC)：用光控制時空
  • Antibody-PROTAC conjugates (Ab-PROTACs)：腫瘤特異性遞送
  • AI 驅動設計：機器學習預測三元複合物形成、linker 設計、ADMET 優化

分子膠 (molecular glue)：單頭分子，誘導兩個原本不互動的蛋白結合。Lenalidomide（cereblon-IKZF1/3）即為經典分子膠，用於多發性骨髓瘤。比 PROTAC 更像「藥物」（小、口服、好遞送）但機制隨機性更高。2024-2025：「分子膠 + PROTAC 混合分子」興起。

Classical inhibitors block function by occupancy; TPD induces target degradation by event-driven mechanisms.

PROTAC (Proteolysis-Targeting Chimera): bifunctional molecule — one end binds target, other binds E3 ligase (cereblon, VHL) → forces proximity → target ubiquitinated → proteasomal degradation. One PROTAC catalyzes multiple degradations.

2025 advances:

  • Trivalent PROTACs: three-point binding gives more stable ternary complex, higher efficiency
  • Novel E3 ligases: beyond cereblon/VHL — RNF114, KEAP1, FEM1B, etc.
  • Photoswitchable PROTACs (PHOTAC): light-controlled spatiotemporal activity
  • Antibody-PROTAC conjugates (Ab-PROTACs): tumor-specific delivery
  • AI-driven design: ML predicts ternary complex formation, linker design, ADMET

Molecular glues: monovalent molecules that induce association between non-interacting proteins. Lenalidomide (cereblon-IKZF1/3) is the classic — used in multiple myeloma. More "drug-like" than PROTACs (small, oral, deliverable) but discovery is more serendipitous. 2024-2025: hybrid "glue-PROTAC" molecules emerging.

⚠️
2025 PROTAC 臨床里程碑：Arvinas 的 ARV-471 (vepdegestrant) 在乳癌 III 期試驗中——是首個進入 III 期的 PROTAC。BMS 的 CC-94676 (KT-474, BTK PROTAC) 等多個 PROTAC 在 II 期。預計 2026-2027 將有首個 PROTAC 獲 FDA 核准。 2025 PROTAC clinical milestone: Arvinas' ARV-471 (vepdegestrant) is in Phase III for breast cancer — the first PROTAC at Phase III. BMS's CC-94676, KT-474 (BTK PROTAC) etc. are in Phase II. First FDA-approved PROTAC expected 2026-2027.
ADMET

五、ADMET

A吸收：Lipinski 5 規則 (MW < 500、LogP < 5、HBD < 5、HBA < 10、可旋轉鍵 < 10)；BCS 分類；P-glycoprotein 外排
D分布：血漿白蛋白結合（影響游離藥比例）；組織分布（脂溶性大者進腦快）
M代謝：肝臟 CYP450（CYP3A4 處理 50% 藥物）、UGT 葡萄醛酸化、N-乙醯化；prodrug 設計
E排泄：腎（小分子、水溶）、膽汁（大分子、脂溶）
T毒性：hERG 心律不整、肝毒（acetaminophen NAPQI）、基因毒、idiosyncratic、混雜效應 (off-target)

🌳 標靶 vs 模式

細胞內標靶→
需穿膜：小分子 / PROTAC / 分子膠 / mRNA。不能用抗體（抗體不過細胞膜）。
細胞表面/外→
抗體、ADC、CAR-T、雙特異抗體都可用。也可考慮小分子（如 GPCR）。
難成藥（無口袋）→
PROTAC / 分子膠（不需高親和力結合）；抑制 RNA 表達（ASO/siRNA）；蛋白-蛋白互作小分子穩定劑/抑制劑。
罕見遺傳病→
基因療法（AAV-）、CRISPR-Cas、ASO、酵素替代（ERT）。
大腦 CNS→
小分子（穿 BBB；MW < 450、LogP 2-5）；少數抗體 (aducanumab, lecanemab 透過血腦屏障少量)；ASO 腦室內注射。
Intracellular→
Needs membrane crossing: small molecules / PROTACs / molecular glues / mRNA. Not antibodies (don't cross membrane).
Surface / extracellular→
Antibodies, ADCs, CAR-T, bispecifics. Also small molecules (e.g. GPCRs).
Undruggable (no pocket)→
PROTAC / molecular glue (no high-affinity binding needed); RNA-level inhibition (ASO/siRNA); PPI stabilizers/inhibitors.
Rare genetic disease→
Gene therapy (AAV), CRISPR-Cas, ASO, enzyme replacement therapy.
CNS→
Small molecules (BBB-permeable; MW < 450, LogP 2-5); few antibodies (aducanumab, lecanemab cross BBB slightly); intrathecal ASOs.

from rdkit import Chem
from rdkit.Chem import Descriptors, AllChem
import requests

# 1) Lipinski filter
def lipinski_pass(smi):
    m = Chem.MolFromSmiles(smi)
    return (Descriptors.MolWt(m) < 500
        and Descriptors.MolLogP(m) < 5
        and Descriptors.NumHDonors(m) <= 5
        and Descriptors.NumHAcceptors(m) <= 10)

candidates = ["CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O",  # aspirin
              "CCN(CC)CCNC(=O)c1ccc(N)cc1",  # procainamide
              "C1=CC=C(C=C1)C(=O)O"]    # benzoic acid
for smi in candidates:
    m = Chem.MolFromSmiles(smi)
    print(f"{Chem.MolToSmiles(m):40s} MW={Descriptors.MolWt(m):.0f} LogP={Descriptors.MolLogP(m):.2f} pass={lipinski_pass(smi)}")

# 2) Docking (AutoDock Vina via Python)
from vina import Vina
v = Vina(sf_name="vina")
v.set_receptor("protein.pdbqt")
v.set_ligand_from_file("ligand.pdbqt")
v.compute_vina_maps(center=[10,5,2], box_size=[20,20,20])
v.dock(exhaustiveness=8, n_poses=10)
print(f"Best ΔG = {v.score()[0]:.1f} kcal/mol")

# Lipinski
lipinski <- function(smi) {
  m <- parse.smiles(smi)[[1]]
  list(
    MW   = get.mol2formula(m)$mass,
    LogP = get.alogp(m),
    HBD  = get.hbond.donor.count(m),
    HBA  = get.hbond.acceptor.count(m)
  )
}
