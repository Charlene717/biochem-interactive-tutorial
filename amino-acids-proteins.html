胺基酸與蛋白質結構

從 20 個字母拼出生命的萬種文字——序列、摺疊、功能的化學邏輯。

Twenty letters spell out the entire vocabulary of life — sequence, folding, and the chemistry of function.

概覽

從胺基酸到「會動的機器」

蛋白質佔細胞乾重的 50% 以上，是酶、結構、受體、運輸、訊息、防禦的執行者。所有蛋白質都是由 20 種標準胺基酸透過肽鍵串接而成。理解胺基酸的化學性質（電荷、極性、體積、芳香性）就能預測：

  • 該蛋白質的等電點 (pI) 與電泳行為
  • 哪些殘基會出現在疏水核心內，哪些在溶劑可及表面
  • 哪些殘基可被磷酸化、乙醯化、糖基化
  • 哪些殘基扮演催化角色（His, Cys, Ser, Asp, Glu, Lys）

Proteins are >50% of cellular dry mass — the executors of catalysis, structure, signaling, transport, and defense. All are built from 20 standard amino acids linked by peptide bonds. Knowing the chemical properties of amino acids (charge, polarity, size, aromaticity) lets you predict:

  • The protein's isoelectric point (pI) and electrophoretic behavior
  • Which residues bury in the hydrophobic core vs the solvent-accessible surface
  • Which residues get phosphorylated, acetylated, or glycosylated
  • Which residues serve as catalytic players (His, Cys, Ser, Asp, Glu, Lys)

💡
記憶訣竅：側鏈 pKa 是預測「該殘基在生理 pH 帶什麼電」的關鍵。Asp/Glu (pKa ~4) 帶負；Lys (pKa 10.5)、Arg (pKa 12.5) 帶正；His (pKa 6.0) 在生理 pH 7.4 半質子化——這就是 His 能在催化中扮演「電子穿梭」角色的原因。 Memory anchor: Side-chain pKa predicts charge at physiological pH. Asp/Glu (pKa ~4) carry negative; Lys (10.5)/Arg (12.5) carry positive; His (pKa 6.0) is half-protonated at pH 7.4 — exactly why it's the "proton shuttle" in countless enzyme active sites.

四級結構

二、蛋白質結構的四個層級

一級結構

胺基酸線性序列。N 端 → C 端的順序。一級結構完全決定後面所有層級（Anfinsen, 1957 Nobel）。突變即在這層改變。

Linear amino-acid sequence, N-terminus → C-terminus. Primary structure fully determines all higher levels (Anfinsen, 1957 Nobel). Mutations alter this layer.

二級結構

α 螺旋 (φ ≈ -60°, ψ ≈ -45°)、β 折疊 (φ ≈ -120°, ψ ≈ +120°)、turns、loops。由骨架氫鍵維持（C=O ⋯ H–N）。預測工具：PSIPRED、DSSP、AlphaFold。

α-helix (φ ≈ -60°, ψ ≈ -45°), β-sheet (φ ≈ -120°, ψ ≈ +120°), turns, loops. Stabilized by backbone H-bonds (C=O ⋯ H–N). Predictors: PSIPRED, DSSP, AlphaFold.

三級結構

整條多肽的 3D 摺疊。由疏水核心、鹽橋、雙硫鍵、氫鍵、van der Waals共同維持。Domain = 可獨立摺疊的結構單元（如 SH2、Ig fold）。

Full 3D fold of a polypeptide. Stabilized by hydrophobic core, salt bridges, disulfides, H-bonds, van der Waals. Domains = independently folding units (SH2, Ig fold, etc.).

四級結構

多個次單元的組合。例如血紅蛋白 α₂β₂、F1-ATP synthase α₃β₃γ。允許協同性（cooperativity）出現——這是 Ch.8 酶調控的基礎。

Assembly of multiple subunits. Examples: hemoglobin α₂β₂, F1-ATP synthase α₃β₃γ. Enables cooperativity — the basis of allosteric regulation (Ch.8).

摺疊與疾病

三、摺疊：能量漏斗與伴隨蛋白

Levinthal 悖論：100 殘基蛋白若隨機搜索構象，需 10⁷⁷ 年才能折成正確結構——但實際只要毫秒。答案是能量漏斗 (folding funnel)：構象空間有定向偏置，朝最低自由能（natively folded）滑落。

Levinthal's paradox: a 100-residue protein randomly sampling conformations would take 10⁷⁷ years to fold — yet it happens in milliseconds. The answer is the folding funnel: conformational space is biased toward the global free-energy minimum (the native fold).

🧬 Hsp70/Hsp90

ATP 驅動的摺疊伴隨蛋白，反覆「黏-放」未摺疊區，給予多次嘗試機會。

ATP-driven chaperones — repeatedly bind/release exposed hydrophobic stretches, giving multiple folding attempts.

📦 GroEL/GroES

桶狀腔體，把不穩定中間體關進「無干擾」環境，給它私下摺疊。

Barrel cavity that sequesters folding intermediates into a "private chamber" away from disruptive cellular crowding.

🩺 錯誤摺疊疾病

Alzheimer Aβ、Parkinson α-synuclein、Huntington poly-Q、prion——同一摺疊出錯就形成澱粉樣纖維。

Alzheimer's Aβ, Parkinson's α-synuclein, Huntington's poly-Q, prion — misfolding leads to amyloid fibrils.

⚠️
2026 觀點：AlphaFold 系列雖能預測「天然結構」，但對 IDP（intrinsically disordered proteins，本質無序蛋白）、構象集合 (conformational ensembles)、變構切換的捕捉仍有限。實驗結構生物學仍不可取代——詳見 Ch.3。 2026 perspective: AlphaFold series predicts native structures well, but still struggles with IDPs (intrinsically disordered proteins), conformational ensembles, and allosteric switches. Experimental structural biology remains irreplaceable — see Ch.3.

四、預測殘基定位與功能

🌳 從序列預測該殘基會在哪裡

該殘基是 Leu/Ile/Val/Phe/Trp？→ 大概率在疏水核心或膜貫穿區。
Q2:
該殘基是 Arg/Lys/Asp/Glu？→ 通常在表面（除非形成內部鹽橋）。
Q3:
該殘基是 Pro？→ 容易出現在turn / loop或α 螺旋開頭 (helix N-cap)。
Q4:
該殘基是 Cys？→ 檢查是否形成雙硫鍵（細胞外蛋白常見、細胞質內罕見）。
Q5:
該殘基是 Gly？→ 提供骨架靈活性，常見於 turn 或活性位點動態區。
Q1:
Is it Leu/Ile/Val/Phe/Trp? → Likely in the hydrophobic core or transmembrane helix.
Q2:
Is it Arg/Lys/Asp/Glu? → Usually on the surface (unless forming an internal salt bridge).
Q3:
Is it Pro? → Often in turns/loops or as α-helix N-cap.
Q4:
Is it Cys? → Check for disulfide bonds (common in extracellular proteins, rare in cytoplasmic ones).
Q5:
Is it Gly? → Provides backbone flexibility — often in turns or dynamic active-site regions.
程式碼

計算 pI 與消光係數

library(Peptides)

seq <- "MASKVAILSAITYRTRDGGVPGKTYTYHHFV"

# 等電點：所有可離子化基團電荷加總 = 0 處
pI(seq, pKscale = "EMBOSS")

# 280 nm 摩爾消光係數 = 1490·#Tyr + 5500·#Trp + 125·#Cys-Cys
# 用於蛋白濃度測定 A280 = ε·c·l
aaComp(seq) |>
  as.data.frame() |>
  dplyr::summarise(eps = 1490*Y + 5500*W)

# 不穩定性指標、GRAVY、aliphatic index
instaIndex(seq)
hydrophobicity(seq, scale = "KyteDoolittle")
from Bio.SeqUtils.ProtParam import ProteinAnalysis

seq = "MASKVAILSAITYRTRDGGVPGKTYTYHHFV"
prot = ProteinAnalysis(seq)

print(f"pI = {prot.isoelectric_point():.2f}")
print(f"MW = {prot.molecular_weight():.1f} Da")
print(f"ε280 = {prot.molar_extinction_coefficient()[0]} M⁻¹·cm⁻¹")
print(f"GRAVY = {prot.gravy():.2f}")         # 疏水性
print(f"Instability = {prot.instability_index():.1f}")
print(f"Aromaticity = {prot.aromaticity():.3f}")
print(f"AA% = {prot.amino_acids_percent}")

# 二級結構傾向（Chou-Fasman 估計）
helix, turn, sheet = prot.secondary_structure_fraction()
print(f"α: {helix:.2f}, β: {sheet:.2f}, turn: {turn:.2f}")
