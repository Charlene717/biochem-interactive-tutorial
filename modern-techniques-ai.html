質譜

代謝體學

二、代謝體

代謝體 = 所有小分子代謝物（< 1500 Da）的集合。2024-2025 臨床熱點：

  • 癌症：oncometabolites（2-hydroxyglutarate in IDH1-mut glioma, fumarate in HLRCC, succinate in pheochromocytoma）；早期偵測（Galleri 多癌篩檢）
  • 神經退化：阿茲海默症血漿磷脂組合預測認知衰退；Parkinson 多巴胺代謝產物
  • 糖尿病：分支鏈胺基酸 (Leu/Ile/Val)、α-hydroxybutyrate 是糖尿病前期指標
  • 心血管：TMAO（腸道菌代謝產物）與動脈硬化關聯
  • 感染症：宿主代謝特徵區分 COVID 嚴重度、結核病潛伏期診斷

MSI 信心等級（Metabolomics Standards Initiative）：Level 1（最高，標品比對）→ Level 4（未知化學式）。臨床用必須 ≥ Level 2。

Metabolome = all small-molecule metabolites (< 1500 Da). 2024-2025 clinical highlights:

  • Cancer: oncometabolites (2-HG in IDH1-mut glioma, fumarate in HLRCC, succinate in pheochromocytoma); early detection (Galleri multi-cancer screen)
  • Neurodegeneration: plasma phospholipid signatures predict Alzheimer's cognitive decline; dopamine metabolites in Parkinson's
  • Diabetes: BCAAs (Leu/Ile/Val), α-hydroxybutyrate as prediabetes markers
  • Cardiovascular: TMAO (gut microbial metabolite) linked to atherosclerosis
  • Infectious disease: host metabolic signatures distinguish COVID severity; latent TB diagnosis

MSI confidence levels: Level 1 (highest, standard match) → Level 4 (unknown formula). Clinical use requires ≥ Level 2.

Cryo

三、Cryo-ET

Cryo-EM（單顆粒）已成主流。Cryo-ET（電子斷層）則是「在細胞裡看蛋白」的革命——把冷凍細胞切薄 (FIB-SEM milling) → 從各角度收集投影 → 重建 3D 體積（解析度 4-10 Å）。已用於：核糖體、剪接體、核孔複合體、突觸結構、病毒組裝中間態。

2025 時間分辨 cryo-EM：雷射閃熔讓樣品融化 5-100 μs，啟動反應後再冷凍——首次能拍下「酶反應中間態」原子級結構。Nature Methods 2025 報導用此技術解析多個 GTPase、kinase 中間態。

Cryo-EM (single-particle) is now mainstream. Cryo-ET (electron tomography) is revolutionizing "in-cell" structural biology — FIB-SEM milling thins frozen cells → projections from many angles → 3D reconstruction (resolution 4-10 Å). Used for ribosomes, spliceosomes, nuclear pore complexes, synaptic structures, viral assembly intermediates.

2025 time-resolved cryo-EM: laser flash-melts samples for 5-100 μs, initiating a reaction before refreezing — the first atomic snapshots of "enzyme reaction intermediates." Nature Methods 2025 reports several GTPase and kinase intermediates resolved this way.

AI

四、AI 在生化學

🤖 ① 結構預測

AF 2 (2020)、AF-Multimer (2022)、AF 3 (Nature 2024)、ESM-3 (2024) 、Boltz-1 (2024 開源 AF3 替代)。提供生化研究的「全球可用結構庫」。

AF 2 (2020), AF-Multimer (2022), AF 3 (Nature 2024), ESM-3 (2024), Boltz-1 (2024 open-source AF3-equivalent). A "global structure library" for biochemistry.

🧬 ② 蛋白設計

RFdiffusion (Baker lab 2023)：擴散模型設計從零開始的蛋白骨架；ProteinMPNN 填入序列。2024-2025 設計出超穩定酶、新發炎抑制劑、奈米孔——速度與成功率史無前例。

RFdiffusion (Baker lab, 2023): diffusion model designs protein backbones de novo; ProteinMPNN fills the sequence. 2024-2025 designs include ultrastable enzymes, novel inflammasome inhibitors, nanopores — unprecedented speed and success.

⚗️ ③ 酶設計

AI 預測活位點殘基、計算過渡態 → 結合定向演化（Arnold 2018 Nobel）。2024 EvolutionaryScale 的 ESM-3 (LM-G)：從 prompts 設計新型發光蛋白、抗體；甚至跨越演化分布的新蛋白。

AI predicts active-site residues, computes transition states → combined with directed evolution (Arnold 2018 Nobel). 2024 EvolutionaryScale's ESM-3 (LM-G): prompts-based design of novel luminescent proteins, antibodies — new proteins beyond known evolutionary distribution.

💊 ④ AI 藥物發現

Insilico Medicine (Pharma.ai)、Recursion、Isomorphic Labs (Google DeepMind 子公司)。2024 第一個 AI 設計 (主要靶點)分子進入 II 期臨床（INS018-055，IPF 治療）。生成式 AI 同時設計分子 + 預測 ADMET。

Insilico Medicine (Pharma.ai), Recursion, Isomorphic Labs (Google DeepMind subsidiary). 2024 first AI-designed (primary target) drug entered Phase II (INS018-055 for IPF). Generative AI simultaneously designs molecules and predicts ADMET.

五、AI vs 實驗

🌳 AI 還是實驗？

已知摺疊類別預測→
AlphaFold 3 / ESM-3 起步——快、便宜、可大規模。但需檢查 pLDDT 與 PAE。
機制細節、配體位置→
實驗結構（X-ray, cryo-EM）為金標準。AI 預測作為起步假設。
IDP、構象動態→
AI 弱項——用 NMR、HDX-MS、SAXS、分子動力學。
新蛋白設計→
RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN 高速產生候選；實驗驗證表達、摺疊、活性。
藥物-靶點親和力→
AF3 + docking 提供方向；最終要 SPR、ITC、cellular assay 確認 Kd 與 IC50。
代謝體鑑定→
AI 加速 m/z 註解；但 MSI Level 1 證據（標品 retention time + MS/MS）仍需實驗。
Known-fold prediction→
Start with AlphaFold 3 / ESM-3 — fast, cheap, scalable. Check pLDDT and PAE.
Mechanism, ligand pose→
Experimental structures (X-ray, cryo-EM) remain the gold standard. AI is the hypothesis starter.
IDP, dynamics→
AI weakness — use NMR, HDX-MS, SAXS, MD simulations.
De novo protein design→
RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN generate candidates fast; experimentally validate expression, fold, activity.
Drug-target affinity→
AF3 + docking suggest poses; final Kd/IC50 needs SPR, ITC, cellular assays.
Metabolite identification→
AI accelerates m/z annotation; MSI Level 1 evidence (standard RT + MS/MS) still requires experiment.

sig = (adj < 0.05).sum()
