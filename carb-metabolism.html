醣類代謝

糖解 10 步 + 糖質新生、肝醣、PPP 與 Warburg 效應——細胞能量學的主軸。

The 10 steps of glycolysis plus gluconeogenesis, glycogen, PPP, and the Warburg effect — the backbone of cellular energetics.

糖解

一、糖解 10 步

葡萄糖 → 2 丙酮酸，發生在細胞質。淨產物：2 ATP + 2 NADH + 2 丙酮酸。分為兩階段：

  • 投資階段（步驟 1–5）：消耗 2 ATP 將葡萄糖活化為 2 個三碳分子
  • 收割階段（步驟 6–10）：產生 4 ATP（淨 2）+ 2 NADH

三個調控節點（不可逆步驟）：己糖激酶（步驟 1，HK）、磷酸果糖激酶 1（步驟 3，PFK-1）、丙酮酸激酶（步驟 10，PK）。

Glucose → 2 pyruvates in the cytoplasm. Net: 2 ATP + 2 NADH + 2 pyruvates. Two phases:

  • Investment (steps 1–5): 2 ATP used to activate glucose into 2 three-carbon molecules
  • Payoff (steps 6–10): 4 ATP made (net 2) + 2 NADH

Three regulatory nodes (irreversible): hexokinase (step 1, HK), phosphofructokinase-1 (step 3, PFK-1), pyruvate kinase (step 10, PK).

PFK-1 是糖解最重要的調控酶。變構抑制：ATP、檸檬酸（細胞能量充足）。變構激活：AMP、ADP（能量不足）、F2,6BP（最強激活劑，由 PFK-2 製造，回應胰島素 / 升糖素）。F2,6BP 系統是 PKL 雙腳激酶/磷酸酶 PFK-2/FBPase-2 機制——詳見 Ch.13。 PFK-1 is the master regulator of glycolysis. Allosteric inhibition: ATP, citrate (energy sufficient). Allosteric activation: AMP, ADP (energy low), F2,6BP (strongest activator, made by PFK-2 in response to insulin/glucagon). The F2,6BP / PFK-2-FBPase-2 bifunctional enzyme is the central hub — see Ch.13.
糖質新生

二、糖質新生

糖質新生 = 非醣前體（乳酸、丙胺酸、甘油、TCA 中間體）合成葡萄糖。發生在肝（90%）與腎（10%）。雖然多數步驟與糖解可逆，但三個不可逆步驟必須繞道：

  1. Pyr → PEP：需要兩步——丙酮酸羧化酶 (PC, 粒線體) → PEP 羧激酶 (PEPCK, 細胞質)，消耗 1 ATP + 1 GTP
  2. F1,6BP → F6P：果糖-1,6-雙磷酸酶 (FBPase-1)
  3. G6P → Glc：葡萄糖-6-磷酸酶 (G6Pase, 內質網)

能量成本：每合成 1 葡萄糖需 6 ATP（糖解只產 2，因此糖質新生「賠錢」——但維持血糖必要）。糖尿病第二型的肝糖質新生失控是血糖升高主因。

Gluconeogenesis = synthesis of glucose from non-carbohydrate precursors (lactate, alanine, glycerol, TCA intermediates) in the liver (90%) and kidney (10%). Most steps share enzymes with glycolysis, but three irreversible glycolysis steps require bypasses:

  1. Pyr → PEP: two steps — pyruvate carboxylase (PC, mitochondrial) then PEP carboxykinase (PEPCK, cytosolic), costing 1 ATP + 1 GTP
  2. F1,6BP → F6P: fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase (FBPase-1)
  3. G6P → Glc: glucose-6-phosphatase (G6Pase, in ER)

Energy cost: 6 ATP per glucose synthesized — gluconeogenesis is "expensive" but essential for blood-glucose maintenance. Type 2 diabetes hepatic gluconeogenesis dysregulation is the main cause of hyperglycemia.

PPP

三、PPP

PPP 是並行於糖解的醣代謝路徑，主要功能：(1) 產生 NADPH（合成代謝與抗氧化）；(2) 產生 核糖-5-磷酸 (R5P)（核苷酸合成的前體）。兩階段：

  • 氧化階段（不可逆）：G6P → 6-磷酸葡萄糖酸內酯 → 6-磷酸葡萄糖酸 → 5-磷酸核酮糖，產生 2 NADPH + 1 CO₂
  • 非氧化階段（可逆）：5C 糖之間相互轉換，產生 F6P 與 G3P 進入糖解，或 R5P 進入核苷酸合成

臨床：G6PD 缺陷是最常見遺傳代謝疾病（~4 億人，X 連鎖）。NADPH 不足無法重生 glutathione，紅血球易受氧化壓力傷害——蠶豆症 (favism)、磺胺類藥物溶血。

PPP runs in parallel with glycolysis. Two main outputs: (1) NADPH for biosynthesis and antioxidant; (2) ribose-5-phosphate (R5P) for nucleotide synthesis. Two phases:

  • Oxidative (irreversible): G6P → 6-phosphogluconolactone → 6-phosphogluconate → ribulose-5-P, yielding 2 NADPH + 1 CO₂
  • Non-oxidative (reversible): interconverts 5C sugars to F6P + G3P (re-enters glycolysis) or R5P (for nucleotides)

Clinical: G6PD deficiency — the most common inherited metabolic disease (~400 M people, X-linked). Without NADPH, glutathione cannot be regenerated; RBCs are vulnerable to oxidative stress — favism and sulfa-drug hemolysis.

Warburg

四、Warburg 效應

⚠️
Otto Warburg 1924 觀察：癌細胞即使在氧氣充足下，仍偏好有氧醣解 (aerobic glycolysis)——葡萄糖代謝為乳酸而非完全氧化。為什麼？癌細胞 ATP 效率（糖解 2 ATP）遠低於 OXPHOS（30 ATP）卻仍選擇糖解。

2024 理解：(1) 快速分裂需要大量合成代謝前體（核苷酸、胺基酸、脂肪酸）來自糖解/PPP 分支；(2) 糖解快速無需粒線體；(3) 釋放乳酸酸化微環境，幫助侵襲與免疫逃逸。2-DG、metformin、GLS 抑制劑等代謝標靶藥物正在臨床測試。 Warburg's 1924 observation: cancer cells prefer aerobic glycolysis even with O₂ — glucose → lactate rather than full oxidation. Why? Cancer ATP efficiency (2 from glycolysis) is far worse than OXPHOS (~30), yet they choose glycolysis.

2024 understanding: (1) rapid division demands biosynthetic precursors (nucleotides, AAs, FAs) from glycolytic/PPP branches; (2) glycolysis is fast and mitochondria-free; (3) released lactate acidifies the tumor microenvironment, aiding invasion and immune escape. Metabolic drugs (2-DG, metformin, GLS inhibitors) are in clinical trials.
決策引導

五、雙向切換

🌳 飯後 vs 飢餓 vs 運動

🍔 飯後→
胰島素↑ → F2,6BP↑ → 激活 PFK-1 / 抑制 FBPase-1 → 糖解 ON、糖質新生 OFF
😴 飢餓→
升糖素↑ → cAMP→PKA → 磷酸化 PFK-2 (FBPase-2 域活化) → F2,6BP↓ → 糖解 OFF、糖質新生 ON
🏃 運動→
AMP↑ → 激活 PFK-1 + AMPK → 糖解 ON；同時抑制 ACC、激活 FA 氧化
🩺 糖尿病→
胰島素阻抗 → 即使飯後仍維持糖質新生 → 高血糖。Metformin 抑制肝糖質新生（標靶 mGPD / GDH）。
🍔 Fed→
Insulin↑ → F2,6BP↑ → activates PFK-1 / inhibits FBPase-1 → glycolysis ON, gluconeogenesis OFF
😴 Fasted→
Glucagon↑ → cAMP→PKA → phosphorylates PFK-2 (activating FBPase-2 domain) → F2,6BP↓ → glycolysis OFF, gluconeogenesis ON
🏃 Exercise→
AMP↑ → activates PFK-1 + AMPK → glycolysis ON; AMPK also inhibits ACC, activating FA oxidation
🩺 Diabetes→
Insulin resistance → gluconeogenesis persists even after meals → hyperglycemia. Metformin inhibits hepatic gluconeogenesis (targets mGPD / GDH).
