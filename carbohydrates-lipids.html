醣類、脂質與生物膜

從單醣到醣綴合物、從脂肪酸到流動鑲嵌膜——生命如何用碳鏈打造能源、結構、界面。

From monosaccharides to glycoconjugates, from fatty acids to fluid-mosaic membranes — how life uses carbon chains to build fuel, structure, and interface.

醣類

一、醣類分類

通式 (CH₂O)ₙ。功能：(1) 能量儲存（澱粉、肝醣）；(2) 結構（纖維素、幾丁質、肽聚醣）；(3) 細胞識別（醣蛋白、醣脂、血型抗原）。重點區分環式 vs 鏈式、α vs β 異頭異構物、D vs L 對映異構。

General formula (CH₂O)ₙ. Functions: (1) energy storage (starch, glycogen); (2) structure (cellulose, chitin, peptidoglycan); (3) cell recognition (glycoproteins, glycolipids, blood-group antigens). Key distinctions: cyclic vs linear, α vs β anomers, D vs L enantiomers.

類別例子化學角色
單醣Glucose, Fructose, Ribose3–7 碳醛醣/酮醣能量、核酸
雙醣Sucrose, Lactose, Maltose醣苷鍵運輸、奶
多醣Glycogen, Cellulose, Starch儲存 vs 結構能源/結構
醣綴合物醣蛋白等N/O 連識別、ECM
💡
糖鏈即訊息：單一蛋白可帶數十種不同醣鏈組合，產生「醣型 (glycoforms)」。ABO 血型即由單醣鏈差異決定（A 加 GalNAc，B 加 Gal，O 不加）。COVID-19 棘蛋白上的 22 個 N-glycans 影響病毒中和抗體辨識——「醣盾」概念。 Glycans are information: a single protein can bear dozens of glycan compositions ("glycoforms"). ABO blood groups are determined by monosaccharide differences (A adds GalNAc, B adds Gal, O nothing). SARS-CoV-2 spike has 22 N-glycans that shield from neutralizing antibodies — the "glycan shield" concept.
脂質

二、脂質類別

🪶 脂肪酸

4–36 碳長鏈羧酸。飽和（無 C=C）vs 不飽和（cis 雙鍵造成扭折，降低熔點）。必需脂肪酸：ω-3 (α-亞麻酸)、ω-6 (亞麻酸)。反式脂肪酸因健康疑慮已被多國禁用。

4–36 C long-chain carboxylic acids. Saturated (no C=C) vs unsaturated (cis double bonds introduce kinks, lowering melting point). Essential FAs: ω-3 (α-linolenic), ω-6 (linoleic). Trans fats are now widely banned for health reasons.

🧈 三酸甘油酯

脂肪細胞主要能源儲存——3 個脂肪酸酯化於甘油。同質量 TG 提供 ATP 是肝醣的 6×。

Main fuel reservoir in adipocytes — 3 FAs esterified to glycerol. TG yields ~6× more ATP per gram than glycogen.

📐 磷脂

膜的主要成分。磷脂醯膽鹼 (PC)、磷脂醯絲胺酸 (PS)、磷脂醯肌醇 (PI/PIP/PIP₂)——PIP₂ 是訊息傳遞關鍵（IP₃/DAG 來源）。

Membrane staples. PC, PS, PI/PIP/PIP₂ — PIP₂ is critical in signaling (source of IP₃/DAG).

🟢 類固醇

四環核（gonane）。膽固醇（膜流動性緩衝）、類固醇激素（皮質醇、性激素、aldosterone）、膽汁酸。

Four-ring gonane core. Cholesterol (membrane fluidity buffer), steroid hormones (cortisol, sex hormones, aldosterone), bile acids.

🌟 鞘脂

以鞘胺醇為骨架，包括鞘磷脂、神經節苷脂（gangliosides）。富集於神經細胞髓鞘與 lipid rafts。Tay-Sachs 病為鞘脂代謝缺陷。

Sphingosine backbone — sphingomyelin, gangliosides. Enriched in nerve myelin and lipid rafts. Tay-Sachs disease is a sphingolipid catabolism defect.

🎯 信號脂質

前列腺素（花生四烯酸代謝產物）、白三烯、內源大麻素。NSAIDs（阿斯匹靈、布洛芬）即抑制 COX 阻斷前列腺素合成。

Prostaglandins (from arachidonic acid), leukotrienes, endocannabinoids. NSAIDs (aspirin, ibuprofen) inhibit COX to block prostaglandin synthesis.

生物膜

三、膜結構與運輸

Singer-Nicolson 流動鑲嵌模型 (1972)：磷脂雙層為動態溶劑，蛋白嵌入或附著。膽固醇調節流動性（高溫時減少、低溫時增加）。Lipid rafts 是富含鞘脂與膽固醇的微結構域，集中訊息傳遞分子。

Singer-Nicolson fluid-mosaic model (1972): the phospholipid bilayer is a dynamic solvent in which proteins are embedded or peripherally associated. Cholesterol buffers fluidity (reduces at high T, increases at low T). Lipid rafts are sphingolipid/cholesterol-rich microdomains that cluster signaling molecules.

四、預測膜運輸

🌳 該分子如何過膜？

Q1:
小、非極性、無電荷（O₂、CO₂、N₂、類固醇）→ 直接簡單擴散，無需轉運蛋白。
Q2:
極性小分子（H₂O、urea、glycerol）→ 透過通道蛋白（aquaporin）。
Q3:
糖、胺基酸→ 順濃度梯度 = GLUT 等促進擴散；逆梯度 = SGLT（共運 Na⁺）。
Q4:
離子（Na⁺、K⁺、Ca²⁺）→ 通道（電壓/配體門控）或 ATPase pump（如 Na⁺/K⁺）。
Q5:
大分子（蛋白、mRNA）→ 內吞 / 外排 / 跨核孔複合體。
Q1:
Small, nonpolar, uncharged (O₂, CO₂, N₂, steroids) → simple diffusion, no transporter.
Q2:
Polar small molecules (H₂O, urea, glycerol) → channel proteins (aquaporin).
Q3:
Sugars, amino acids → down gradient = GLUT facilitated; up gradient = SGLT (Na⁺ symport).
Q4:
Ions (Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺) → channels (voltage/ligand-gated) or ATPase pumps (Na⁺/K⁺).
Q5:
Macromolecules (proteins, mRNA) → endocytosis / exocytosis / nuclear pore complexes.

for name, smi in lipids.items():
    m = Chem.MolFromSmiles(smi)
    print(f"{name:20s} MW={Descriptors.MolWt(m):6.1f} LogP={Descriptors.MolLogP(m):5.2f}")

# 預測膜通透性（Lipinski + LogP）
def likely_permeates(smiles):
    m = Chem.MolFromSmiles(smiles)
    MW = Descriptors.MolWt(m); logP = Descriptors.MolLogP(m)
    HBD = Descriptors.NumHDonors(m); HBA = Descriptors.NumHAcceptors(m)
    return (MW < 500) and (logP < 5) and (HBD <= 5) and (HBA <= 10)
